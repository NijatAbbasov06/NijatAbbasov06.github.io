GNSS-Denied UAV Geo-Localization with VIO Fusion

[Code on GitHub] Aug 2026

  • Built a CPU-only system that bounds visual-inertial odometry (VIO) drift with absolute fixes from satellite imagery, fusing OpenVINS (MSCKF) with self-supervised cross-view geo-localization (CAEVL).
  • Rejected outliers with chi-square gating against the VIO prior and consensus checks, raising fix precision from 17.7% to 99%.
  • Reduced position RMSE by 36% in real time (13.3 Hz on CPU) on 3.6 km of real flight data, and by 71% over the cruise segment offline.

OpenVINS, ROS 2, Kalibr, PyTorch, Python

Multi-Disciplinary Robotics: Autonomous Drone Navigation
